# Bubu Games

A handful of little games about two red pandas: Yier, the white one, and Bubu, the brown one. Open the app and pick a game.

## Grab the Buns

Race a friend for buns. One phone opens a room and shows a code; the other scans it and joins. You can also practice on your own against the computer. The left stick moves, the right one drops a sugar bubble. Set a nickname and pick a look before you play.

## Merge Little Bubu

Drop Bubu into the box. Two of the same kind blend into a bigger one. The round ends when the pile crosses the line.

## Off to See Your Little Happiness

Play as Bubu or Yier. Walk through the city, the park, and a starry sky, and pick up the small happy moments along the way.

## Chinese Chess

Play red against the computer. Choose beginner, intermediate, or master. You can take back a move, switch the board style, or open a saved game and step through it.
